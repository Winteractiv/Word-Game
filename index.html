Scoring Rules

Pink tiles (Q, W, Z, X, V, K, J) are worth 5 points each and are rarer than other letters: Every other letter is worth 1. Also, 3-letter words are valid if they contain a pink tile.
⭐ Joker tiles act as any letter — the game automatically picks whichever letter scores highest. Jokers are worth 1 point and appear slightly more often than other tiles.
Word score = (sum of letter values) × word length². Longer words score exponentially more!
Spelling 2+ separate words (different rows/columns) in the same turn adds a +40 bonus, added before multipliers.
Each time you clear letters by spelling a word, a random empty cell gains a multiplier (×2, ×3, or ×4). Higher values appear near the edges. Multipliers last 8 turns and show a countdown. Multipliers are applied after all other point bonuses.
💣 Bombs allow you to remove tricky letters from your board. Bombs score 10 points per letter removed (multipliers apply).
